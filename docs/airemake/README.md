Status 2026-09-26 HDEF overheat: the generator's overheat path was fired for the first time, closing the item the in-game manual left open when it named hdef.fault as a state nothing had been observed to set. Heat reaches 100 at t=99 of Running with the fans off, the three C-Pumps on and the generator switched on; line 174 faults it and heat then decays at HDEFHeatLoss 2 per second. The pumps are what buy the time, because with them idle the shift dies at 91 seconds on peaStress 100 instead, about nine seconds short of the overheat. A zero-input shift fails at 102 seconds with Reactor stallout, and that is not a defect: manual item 31 instructs the operator to disable the outtake fans periodically when in State 1 to avoid stallout, and the fans are the only control that reverses the sign of the pressure derivative at startup temperature, so any longer-running shift necessarily had them off. Nothing in the engine was changed.
